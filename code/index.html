HOW TO CONNECT
  • Smile · It warms, opens up and comes closer.
  • Sad look · It turns blue, slows and gathers.
  • Surprise · It turns purple, opens outward and settles.
  • Sound · Your volume creates ripples. A sudden loud sound scatters it once, then it gathers again.
  • Demo gestures · Slow movement invites it to follow. Open to expand, close to gather, move quickly to startle it.
  • Demo body movement · More movement brings a bigger sway.

Gentle interaction builds familiarity. Reset clears Demo inputs. Start fresh resets familiarity.

Live uses your microphone and MediaPipe Face Landmarker on this device. Facial movements are approximate cues, not a measure of your feelings. Hiding the preview keeps tracking on. Home or End interaction stops both devices.

Demo is fully simulated. Hand and body tracking and ElevenLabs are not connected. Capture saves only the particle image, never the camera.
