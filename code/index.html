    HOW TO CONNECT
    
  • Smile · It warms, opens up and comes closer.
  • Sad look · It turns blue, slows and gathers.
  • Surprise · It turns purple, opens outward and settles.
  • Sound · Your volume creates ripples. A sudden loud sound scatters it once, then it gathers again.
  • Hand gestures · Move slowly to guide it. Open your palm to expand, close your hand to gather, or wave quickly to startle it. Pause before waving again.
  • Sudden approach · Hold steady, then bring your face or palm closer quickly. It shrinks and tucks toward the centre, then gently opens again. Slow approaches stay calm. Pause before trying again.
  • Body movement · Sway from side to side, or raise and open both arms to help it float and expand. Moving adds rotation and flow; pausing lets it settle. Body movement alone never startles it.

    
Simply stay in view. A quiet minute or two brings it closer; smiles and gentle sounds help it warm up sooner. A neutral or sad look never reduces familiarity. A loud sound may startle it, but does not undo your bond.

    
If your face leaves the frame, growth pauses and your progress stays. After about eight seconds away it gradually rests. Growth pauses in background tabs. Demo presence uses the same timing. Reset clears Demo inputs; Start fresh and Home reset familiarity.
